Agentic Workflow Impact Model

A Quantitative Venture Thesis on Enterprise AI Integration

Author: Dhruv Jani • Summer 2026

Executive Summary

The public market is flooded with announcements of autonomous AI agents and enterprise generative AI rollouts. This project cuts through the PR noise using rigorous Event Study Methodology and Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression to empirically determine which AI integrations actually drive statistically significant valuation increases versus those dismissed as mere hype.

The Methodology

  • Data Ingestion: Automated fetching of historical adjusted close prices via the Yahoo Finance API.
  • Expected Returns: Calculated using a 252-day OLS regression window against the NASDAQ benchmark (^IXIC).
  • Impact Estimation: Computes the Cumulative Abnormal Return (CAR) over a localized event window to quantify net market sentiment.

Key Findings: The AI Value Drivers

By isolating the market reaction from broader macroeconomic movements, the model identified the following top-performing enterprise AI rollouts out of 15 tracked events:

MNDY

+8.51% CAR

Monday AI rollout strongly validated as a core driver for new enterprise seats and retention.

ADBE

+3.03% CAR

Firefly generative AI rewarded as a highly effective protective moat for creative workflows.

BOX

+0.41% CAR

Flat reaction; Box AI largely priced in as table stakes for modern cloud storage platforms.
